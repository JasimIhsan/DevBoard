The **Backlog** is where work starts. Capture ideas and upcoming tasks here, then move a task onto the board when you are ready to work on it. Finished tasks you no longer want on the board end up in **Archived**, right below the backlog.

## The flow

1. **Add** tasks to the backlog.
2. **Move to board** when you are ready to start one.
3. **Drag** it across the board's columns as the work progresses.
4. When it is **Done**, **archive** it to keep the board tidy.

## Add tasks to the backlog

Type a title in the box at the bottom of the backlog and press `Enter`, or use the **New task** button (new tasks go to the backlog by default). Tasks are sorted by priority, highest first.

You can also bring many tasks in at once with [Import from CSV](/docs/import-csv).

## Move tasks to the board

- Click **Move to board** on a task. It lands at the bottom of the first **To Do** column.
- Open the **⋯** menu and choose **Move to board…** to pick a specific column.

### Select and move several tasks

- **Tick** the checkbox at the start of a row. The checkbox in the header selects every task.
- **Shift-click** a second checkbox to select a range.
- **Ctrl/⌘-click** a task's title to add or remove it without opening it.

A bar appears with **Move to board**, **Move to…** (any column), **Priority** and **Clear**. `Esc` clears the selection.

## Archived tasks

Done tasks can be archived. They disappear from the board, stay **Done**, and are listed in the **Archived** section below the backlog.

- **Archive** one task with the archive button on its card (visible when you hover a Done card, always on phones) or **Archive** in its detail panel.
- **Archive everything done** from a Done column's **⋯** menu.
- **Restore** brings a task back onto the board, into its Done column. Moving an archived task to any other column also restores it.

> **Note** Only done tasks can be archived. Archiving never changes a task's status: it is still done, just out of sight.

## Send a task back

Open a task and set **Column** to **Backlog**, or use the **Column** choice in its detail panel. It leaves the board and returns to the backlog.

**To-dos** are a personal checklist for quick things: "call the bank", "review Sam's PR", "buy cables". They are separate from project tasks, so they don't need a project, sprint or status.

## When to use a to-do

| Use a **to-do** for | Use a **task** for |
| --- | --- |
| Small personal reminders. | Work that belongs to a project. |
| Things you tick off and forget. | Work you plan, estimate and track on a board. |
| Anything that doesn't need a sprint. | Anything with subtasks, comments or a branch. |

## Open your to-dos from anywhere

| Where | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Desktop** | The checklist icon in the top bar. A badge shows how many are open. |
| **Phone** | The round checklist button at the bottom right of every page. |
| **Keyboard** | Press `T` (when not typing in a field). |
| **Command palette** | Press `⌘K`, then choose **Open to-dos**. |
| **Sidebar** | **To-dos** under *All projects board*. |

The drawer slides in over whatever you are doing. Use the expand icon in its header to open the full **To-dos** page.

## Add a to-do

1. Open the drawer. The cursor is already in the box.
2. Type and press `Enter` (or tap **+**).
3. Keep typing. The box stays focused, so you can add several in a row.

To give it a due date, tap the calendar icon in the box before you add it. You can also add or change a date later from the to-do itself.

## Manage to-dos

- **Complete:** tick the circle. Finished items move to **Completed**.
- **Edit:** tap the text, change it and press `Enter`. `Esc` cancels.
- **Due date:** tap the date to change it, or **Add date** to set one. Overdue dates turn red and dates within two days turn amber.
- **Delete:** the trash icon (always visible on phones, shown on hover on desktop).
- **Clear completed:** open **Completed** and choose **Clear** to delete every finished to-do.

> **Note** There are no accounts, so to-dos are shared by every device that opens this app, just like *Recent*. They sync when you open the app or refresh.

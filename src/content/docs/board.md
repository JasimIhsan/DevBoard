The **Board** is a Kanban view of your project. Each column is a stage of work, and each card is a task. Only tasks you have moved from the backlog appear here.

## Default columns

Every project starts with three columns:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **To Do** | Planned work that has not started. |
| **In Progress** | Work being done now. |
| **Done** | Finished work. |

Each column also shows how many cards it holds and their total points.

## Move cards

Drag a card to another column, or reorder it within its column. The change saves immediately and the card stays where you dropped it. Order is kept, so the top card is your next priority.

- **Keyboard:** focus a card, press `Space` to pick it up, use the arrow keys to move it and `Space` again to drop.
- **Phones:** the board shows one column at a time with tabs to switch. Tap the **⇄** icon on a card to move it to any column.

## Add your own columns

Projects can have more stages than the defaults, for example **In Review**, **QA** or **Blocked**.

1. Click **Add column** (above the board, or at the right end of the columns).
2. Give it a name and choose what it **means**.
3. Click **Add column**.

### What a column "means"

Every column has a type so DevBoard knows how to treat its tasks, whatever you call it:

| Type | Use it for | What it affects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To do** | Work waiting to start. | Counts as not started. |
| **In progress** | Any stage of active work: review, QA, blocked. | Counts as in progress on the dashboard. |
| **Done** | Finished states. | Stamps the completion time and allows [archiving](/docs/backlog). |

### Manage a column

Open the **⋯** menu on a column header to:

- **Rename or change type.** Tasks inside follow the new meaning.
- **Move left / Move right** to reorder the columns.
- **Archive all** (Done columns): hides every task in it. See [Backlog & archive](/docs/backlog).
- **Delete column.** If it has tasks, you choose another column to move them to. A board always keeps at least one column.

## Filters

The bar above the board narrows the cards:

- **Filter tasks…** searches titles and keys.
- **Priority**, **Type** and **Label** dropdowns limit the cards shown.

Filters only affect what you see. They never change your tasks.

## Archive from the board

Hover a card in a Done column and click the archive icon, or use **Archive all** in the column menu. Archived tasks are listed below the [backlog](/docs/backlog).

## When the board is empty

A new project's board is empty until you move tasks in. Add tasks in the [Backlog](/docs/backlog), then use **Move to board**.

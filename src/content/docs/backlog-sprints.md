The **Backlog** page is where you plan. It lists every sprint and the unscheduled backlog on one screen.

## The backlog

Tasks that are not in a sprint live here. Add them with the quick-add row, prioritise them by dragging, and estimate them so you can plan realistically.

## Creating a sprint

Choose **Create sprint** and enter:

- **Name**, such as *Sprint 4*.
- **Goal** (optional), a one-line statement of what the sprint should achieve.
- **Dates** (optional). They default to today and two weeks later when you start it.

## Adding work to a sprint

Drag tasks from the backlog into the sprint, or set the **Sprint** field on a task. The sprint header totals the points so you can see whether you are over-committing.

## Starting a sprint

Click **Start sprint**. The sprint becomes **Active** and its tasks appear in *To Do* on the [Board](/docs/board).

> **Note** Only one sprint per project can be active. Complete the current sprint before starting another.

## Completing a sprint

Click **Complete sprint** on the board. A dialog lists any unfinished tasks and asks where they should go:

- **Backlog** (default), or
- **Another draft sprint**.

Done tasks stay in the completed sprint so you keep a history of what shipped.

## Editing and deleting sprints

You can rename a sprint or change its goal and dates at any time. Draft and completed sprints can be deleted; their unfinished tasks return to the backlog. The active sprint must be completed first.
